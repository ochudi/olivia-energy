## Overview

Olivia Energy and Power Company Limited is a strategic energy advisory, research
and sustainability consultancy founded by Dr Olugbenga Olaoye, an energy
economist who writes for BusinessDay. The site is read by energy ministries,
regulators, investors, development partners and research institutes, and by the
journalists who quote him. It has to do one thing a stranger can repeat after a
visit: Olivia measures the gap, then bridges it.

The look comes from his world, not from a style. Nigerian light: the Third
Mainland Bridge at last light, street lamps coming on in Abuja, the flare at
Obunagha at dawn. Infrastructure that installs but does not deliver: a grid of
13,625 MW that runs at a third, drawn as squares that go dark. Fuel stations,
where the firm began in 2020. His op-eds, set as report covers in the logo's
colours. His data, on plates with their sources, tables and CSVs. The logo is a
flame of lime and red rising out of a green ring, and the whole system is cut
from it: its three colours, the ring's one large corner, the flame as a list
marker, the ring that closes at the end of every page.

The system is bold on purpose. The firm asked for presence, not restraint. Boldness is spent
in one place per page (the signature moment) and the rest of the page holds
still so that it lands.

## Colors

Six colours with jobs. Three are the mark's own; the other three are what the
mark is set on.

- **Green `#047A36` (the stage).** Flood surface for the firm's statements and
  for Why Olivia; the colour of primary buttons, links and the glyphs on the
  field. The mark itself is always its exact green `#04923F`, never the flood.
  White text on green 5.5:1.
- **Red `#D5281B` (the gap).** The hatch on every chart, the one solid red
  pill that names a gap, the red flood of the close band on every page, and
  the big quotation flame. Red means the gap and nothing else. Small red text
  on the field uses red ink `#B8231A` (5.7:1).
- **Lime `#88C029` (the evidence).** The mark's tongue. It earns its place as
  the colour of the latest evidence: the lead line on a night chart, the lead
  cover on the wall, the lime flood under the numbers band, the second line of
  the tagline in the footer. Never text on the field, green or red. Ink on
  lime 7.9:1.
- **Night `#06170E` (the plate).** A green-black, not neutral black. It is one
  of five surfaces, never the page: figure plates, the founder portrait band,
  the About hero (where the mark itself is the subject), the latest-op-ed card
  and the footer. Prose never sits on night. On night, lime is never alone:
  the red hatch or the paper-white series is always beside it. Never two
  night slabs in a row. Panels and grid lines on night are `#0E2418`.
- **Field `#F1F3EA` (the ground).** White with the mark's green at about five
  per cent: a cool, green-cast field rather than a warm cream, so even the
  ground is the logo's. Ink `#0B1F14` on it 15.4:1; muted ink `#4A5A50`
  6.5:1; green text 4.9:1. The deep band `#E6EBDC` alternates sections and
  grounds the cover wall; on it, secondary text uses muted ink only (subtle
  ink falls to 4.5:1).
- **Ink `#0B1F14`.** Text on the field and on lime. White `#FFFFFF` is the
  text on green, red, night and photographs.

Surfaces: every section declares one of field, field-deep, green, lime, red,
night or photo, and the surface remaps the semantic tokens (canvas, ink, line,
primary, chart series, hatch) so components never carry a per-surface variant.
One flood per section, carrying one statement. Photographs sit under a night
scrim strong enough for 4.5:1 body text.

## Typography

Two faces, already self-hosted and subset; no new family, no new weight.

- **Newsreader, weight 400, roman.** Every headline, the Why Olivia lines, the
  numerals, the quotations, the cover titles, the "Fig. n" caption. The optical
  size axis gives the 150 px home headline and the 20 px cover title different
  cuts of the same face.
- **Newsreader italic, in one place.** The second line of the firm's tagline,
  "Inspiring sustainable futures.", on the home hero and in the footer. That
  line is the turn of his phrase, so it turns. Italic is never a word or a
  sentence inside any other heading or statement.
- **Instrument Sans, 400 to 600.** Body, ledes, lists, buttons, chips, sources,
  navigation, chart labels. Chosen against Inter and Fraunces; the reasons are
  in `src/app/fonts.ts`.

The scale is the existing one in `src/styles/tokens.css`: display-2xl (home H1
only, two words), display-xl for statements of six words or fewer, display-lg
for every H1 that is a sentence and for section H2s, display-md to xs for
cards, lines and titles, numeral for the big numbers, and the fixed text sizes
17, 15 and 12 px. Body is never below 15 px except captions and source lines.

Labels: uppercase, tracked, 12 px text appears in two places only, table heads
and chart axes. Nothing sits above a heading unless it carries information the
heading does not, and then it is a sentence in the body face: "11 op-eds,
quoted in 12 BusinessDay reports", "Latest in BusinessDay, 10 Sep 2026". Lists
are joined with commas and line breaks, never with middle dots. Numbers are
numbered only when they are a sequence (the three steps of Fig. 2, the years
on the timeline). Counts in prose are words up to twenty.

## Layout

Twelve columns, 24 px gaps from 1024 px; eight columns at 768; four at 390. The
page container is 1296 px; the gutter runs from 20 px at 390 to 72 px at 1440.
Section rhythm is 80 to 160 px top and bottom, bands inside a section 56 to
96 px. Headings with a lede split seven and five across the grid; the lede
reads at most 42 characters per line, body at most 68.

The structural idea: each page is a sequence of floods, one surface at a time,
and one of them is the page's signature moment. Phones get the same content in
one column, with native horizontal rails for the service cards and the cover
wall.

One signature moment per page, everything else quiet:

- **Home** leads with the Third Mainland Bridge at last light, then the green
  floods in and the photograph survives only inside the mark while the nine
  Why Olivia lines light.
- **What we do** leads with the grid that installs but does not deliver: the
  pinned plate of 136 squares that go dark, then red, as the reader scrolls
  through chapter two.
- **About** leads with the mark igniting out of sparks in its three colours on
  capable desktops, and drawing itself in strokes everywhere else.
- **Insights** leads with his op-eds as a wall of report covers that assemble
  as the reader arrives at them.
- **Research** leads with the 2022 to 2026 timeline of his articles and op-eds
  drawing itself across a night plate.
- **Contact** leads with the Africa map settling on Southwest Nigeria.
- Credits, Privacy, the article pages and the 404 lead with their text and have
  no moment.

## Elevation & Depth

None. A single 1 px line in the surface's line colour is the only edge
treatment; the night plate is the only lift, and it is tonal, not a shadow. No
shadows, no glow, no glass, no gradients other than photo scrims. Depth on the
site is done with surfaces (a night plate on lime, a card on the field) and
with the photographs' own depth.

## Shapes

The shoulder: three corners of 4 px and one large top-right corner that echoes
the ring of the mark. It is the firm's shape, so it goes on the firm's own
objects and nowhere else.

- **Shoulder-lg (64 to 240 px):** photographs and flood panels (vision and
  mission).
- **Shoulder (40 px):** op-ed covers, the four flood tiles (Governments,
  Private sector, Development partners, Academia), the latest-op-ed card.
- **Instrument (4 px):** everything that measures or takes input. Figure
  plates, numeral cards, the contact form and its fields, journal cards,
  format tiles, the author card, the quotation panel, tables.
- **Pill:** buttons and chips. Buttons carry their label only; no arrow. The
  ring arrow survives as the external-link marker on op-ed covers and journal
  cards, where the link leaves the site and the ring is already the mark.
- **Circles** only where the ring is meant: chart end markers, the founder's
  portrait (a circular crop, face centred, framed by the ring), the closing
  ring, the region ring on the map.

Forbidden: arches, notches, die-cuts, offset or drop shadows, glassmorphism,
decorative gradients.

## Components

Shared on every page, unchanged: the header (logo lockup, four links, the
"Start a conversation" pill, recolouring to the surface beneath it); the red
close band (statement in display-xl, the ring that closes, the button); the
night footer (the two-line tagline, company details in two lines, three link
columns, the credits line). The figure plate (caption-style "Fig. n" and title,
chart, reading, source with retrieval date, "View as table", "Get the data
(CSV)"). The op-ed cover in lead, standard and compact sizes. The numeral tile
(final numeral, tag chip, caption, glyph, source). The 14 line glyphs built
only from the ring, the tongue, straight strokes and the dot, in the surface's
glyph colour, never filled. The flame bullet as the list marker.

### Motion

- One signature moment per page (Layout). Everything else is still.
- Scroll effects are native CSS scroll-driven animations (`animation-timeline:
view()` or `scroll()`) behind `@supports`; the final state is the fallback.
- Three reveals, tied to content type and nothing else: `draw` for data (chart
  lines, the hatch, glyphs, the ring), `wipe` for photographs, and none for
  text. Headings, ledes, lists and cards are static.
- The founder's ring is the one time-based draw: it draws once as the portrait
  enters the viewport, then stays still. Never scroll-linked, never looping;
  reduced motion and no JavaScript show the finished ring.
- Numerals never count up. The glyph beside a number is what moves.
- Parallax only on photography, as a scroll-driven transform, at most 10 per
  cent of the image height; never on text, never on charts.
- Page to page: React `ViewTransition` per the Next 16 guide, carrying the logo
  and the headline between routes; instant navigation where unsupported.
- Hover and magnetic effects only under `(hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)`.
- No smooth-scroll or scroll-hijacking library; no animation dependency ships
  to visitors. `prefers-reduced-motion` and no JavaScript render the final
  state. Scripting under 2 ms a frame at 4x CPU throttle.
- Durations and easings are the tokens in `src/styles/tokens.css`.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do reuse the header, close band, footer and tokens on every page, unchanged.
- Do add a token to `src/styles/tokens.css` before a page uses it. Never
  invent a one-off inside a component.
- Do keep every number traceable to `src/content/data/facts.ts`, a series file
  or the publication data, with its source on its face.
- Do keep copy in `src/content`, British spelling, no em or en dashes.
- Don't change: the firm's tagline and its two-line setting; the mark's
  three colours; Newsreader and Instrument Sans; the shoulder; the red hatch;
  the graded photographs and their credits; the figures' sources, tables and
  CSVs.
- Don't use: a warm cream ground; night with lime as the page rather
  than as a plate; tracked uppercase labels above headings and middle-dot
  meta strings; an italic or coloured word or sentence inside a headline;
  decorative 01 / 02 / 03 on anything that is not a sequence; arrows on
  buttons; count-up numbers; the same fade on every block; one radius on every
  surface; shadows, glow or glass.
